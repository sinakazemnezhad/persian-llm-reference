## What PLR adds (without replacing others)

| Layer | Role | Examples we honor and link |
|-------|------|----------------------------|
| **Discovery** | Curated links, papers, repos | [Awesome-Persian-LLM](https://github.com/MohammadHeydari/Awesome-Persian-LLM) |
| **Scores** | Model rankings on specific tasks | MIZAN, PartAI, Matina leaderboards |
| **Atlas** | Typed records, verification gates, bilingual JSON | **PLR** — this repo |
| **Build** | Corpus, training, measured weights | Separate programs (e.g. Alefbâ) |

We credit the people and projects that came first. PLR's job is to **organize, source, and verify** — not to rank ourselves above them.

## Completeness definition (v1.0)

- **40+** indexed entries across models · datasets · benchmarks · leaderboards
- **Every** major open Persian instruct model under 15B represented with primary URL
- **Every** major Persian eval suite linked with paper URL
- **Bilingual** FA default + EN parity on all UI strings
- **Machine API** stable for agents (`reference-manifest.json` + well-known)
- **Monthly** changelog with `generatedAt` bump
- **Compare** filters: class · status · size · axes

## Tone law

```text
OPEN · SOURCED · RESPECTFUL — WE MAP THE FIELD WITH SCIENTISTS AND BUILDERS, NOT AGAINST THEM.
```

- No "best", "beats", or "wins" unless a **measured** benchmark receipt exists for that row.
- Null and unknown stay honest — we do not invent superiority.
- Contributors and upstream maintainers get named credit in entries and outreach.
