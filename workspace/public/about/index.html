About Persian LLM Reference

Maintained by Persian LLM Reference maintainers. Independent reference and evidence infrastructure for the Persian (Farsi) language-model ecosystem.

PLR maps claims to public evidence. Unsupported claims remain explicitly unverified. We do not rank models or certify products.
